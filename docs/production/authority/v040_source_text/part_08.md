AS. Cosmology and Dimensional Structure
STATUS: CURRENT AUTHORITATIVE BASELINE. The cosmology is intentionally extensible and capable of
escalating into increasingly complex metaphysical structures without declaring any currently known
layer to be the final totality.

AS.1 Reality hierarchy is layered but graph-like
   The baseline graph of Universes, Dimensions, Worlds and Sub-realms remains authoritative; reality
    is not forced into one simple rigid nesting tree.
   Sub-realms are bounded subordinate realities such as pockets, sealed zones, afterlife regions,
    dungeon-realms, inner worlds or local reality layers.
   Worlds are major self-consistent realities with their own environment, history, laws and World
    Rank.
   Dimensions are higher-order metaphysical envelopes/networks able to contain, connect or sustain
    multiple Worlds/Sub-realms that share deeper structural laws.
   Universes are broader continuities that may contain or intersect multiple Dimensions.
   The Interstice is the higher medium/network through which otherwise distinct
    Universes/Dimensions may be related, separated, reached or isolated.
   The Outer Beyond is an intentionally open category for reality that does not belong to the ordinary
    Interstice/dimensional structure.
   Future content may discover higher or lateral structures beyond the Interstice/Outer Beyond model
    if it preserves prior causal rules and stable persistent history.

AS.2 Dimensional metaphysical consciousness
   Dimensions are semi-conscious only in a metaphysical sense. They are not normally people,
    conversational minds or human-like planners.
   A Dimension can express a distributed homeostatic will through structural self-preservation,
    resistance to incompatible laws, reactions to existential threats, stabilization of Worlds, pressure
    against over-limit beings and long-horizon preparation mechanisms.
   This 'consciousness' is closer to an immune/homeostasis function of reality than to a character
    personality.
   Rare avatars, prophets, interfaces or emergent intelligences may interpret dimensional impulses, but
    such intermediaries are distinct from proving that the underlying Dimension itself thinks like a
    person.

AS.3 World Rank
   World Rank is distinct from personal Existence/Power Rank.
   It represents the World's stable reality capacity: law integrity/complexity, energy ceiling,
    dimensional anchoring, ability to sustain high-Rank beings/phenomena, metaphysical density,
    resilience to reality alteration and connected higher structures.
   World Rank does not directly decide combat. It defines what scale of existence and reality
    manipulation the World can naturally host without severe distortion.

AS.4 World Rank can rise
   World Rank may increase when the World's own structure actually evolves rather than merely
    because one strong individual lives there.
   Possible causes include long-term high-grade energy accumulation, mature Domain/Core anchors,
    world-scale technology/magic/cultivation, stable presence of high-Rank existences,
    dimensional/world fusion, absorption of compatible realms, major metaphysical infrastructure,
    major historical transformations or deliberate world-evolution mechanisms.
   An evolved World may open new Junctions, host stronger existences with less suppression, develop
    new resources/ecology/laws and become visible to higher-order powers.

AS.5 World Rank can decline
   World Rank may also fall temporarily or permanently when foundational structure is damaged.
   Possible causes include dimensional wounds, destruction of major anchors, collapse/corruption of
    multiple Domains/Cores, foundational energy drain, failed large-scale fusion, fragmentation,
    catastrophic war, external predation, assimilation or deliberate sealing.
   Decline does not automatically mean total extinction. A World may remain inhabited while
    becoming weaker, unstable, fragmented, isolated or less capable of supporting high-Rank
    phenomena.
   Restoration remains possible when the actual damaged structures/laws can be repaired.

AS.6 World pressure and Junctions
   Personal Rank, World Rank and dimensional compatibility interact but remain separate systems.
   A being above local World tolerance may enter through a valid route; increasing mismatch can
    cause power suppression, higher costs, unstable manifestation, reality resistance, law conflict,
    environmental damage or dimensional rejection effects.
   Vessels, avatars, self-sealing, Domains, dimensional infrastructure, compatible bodies and authored
    abilities can mitigate such pressure.
   Junctions are causal links between reality nodes rather than menu-only unlocks. Requirements may
    involve World Rank, personal Rank, law compatibility, knowledge/coordinates, keys, infrastructure,
    Authority, timing, dimensional events, energy cost or story/world conditions.
   Irregular breaches can bypass normal compatibility and expose the player to content far beyond the
    current safe range.

AS.7 Dimensional preparation / gacha-contract mechanism
   The dimensional contract/gacha preparation system is primarily a metaphysical homeostatic defense
    mechanism of Dimensions.
   It does not require a speaking deity or conscious administrator.
   A Dimension can expose mechanisms that connect compatible Character Identities to Rulers,
    instantiate Ruler-specific Manifestations, encourage accumulation/development of power and
    strengthen territorial/Domain defenses against long-horizon existential danger.
   This is distributed preparation, not proof that the protagonist is the sole chosen hero. NPC Rulers can
    use the same broad systems.
   The exact historical origin and any higher entity/civilization involvement remain spoiler-protected
    authored lore.

AS.8 Character Identity access
   Acquisition does not remove a finite physical person from another Ruler or exhaust a universal body
    pool.
   The dimensional system accesses a higher-order Identity Pattern/Record associated with the
    canonical Character Identity and instantiates a Ruler-specific Manifestation under the established
    Identity -> Version -> Manifestation ontology.
   The Pattern is a metaphysically valid existence-template, not merely a software database copy.
   Different Rulers may therefore possess divergent Manifestations of the same Character Identity; the
    same Ruler may also possess multiple divergent Manifestations as already defined for character
    development.
   Identity access can be constrained by banners, sealed packages, dimensional compatibility/reach,
    world state and other explicit rules.

AS.9 Endgame threat ecology
   True endgame threats are not one mandatory species or faction.
   Threats may originate outside the ordinary dimensional structure or from within it through collapse,
    corruption, transformation or runaway evolution.
   Supported broad families include dimensional predators, assimilators, law-eroders, conceptual
    parasites, self-propagating reality infestations, collapsed-Dimension remnants, hostile high-order
    civilizations/systems and entities for which ordinary matter/space/time are only incidental
    constraints.
   The taxonomy is deliberately data-driven and expandable so virtually infinite future content does
    not have to reduce to one enemy type.
   Threats may possess their own Existence hierarchies, territory structures, law/Authority equivalents,
    personal-reality fields and dimensional infrastructure.
   They remain causal entities rather than hidden scaling devices: Rank, Authority, knowledge,
    counters, compatibility and explicit bypass mechanics still govern interactions.

AS.10 Infinite-expansion rule
   The game never needs to declare the currently known highest cosmological structure to be
    objectively final.
   Future higher structures are valid when they explain their relationship to lower layers, preserve
    established causal behavior, do not retroactively trivialize earlier progression without a real
    mechanism and use stable/versioned content definitions.
   This permits increasingly complex HI3-like metaphysical escalation while keeping one coherent
    persistent save.

AS.11 Spoiler boundary
   The architecture above may be documented openly.
   Exact oldest structures, the true historical origin of the preparation system, the protagonist's origin,
    named highest threats, causes of ancient collapses and hidden links between Dimensions/Identities
    remain spoiler-protected narrative material unless the Creative Director explicitly asks to inspect
    them.

AT. Protagonist Higher-Order Progression
STATUS: CURRENT AUTHORITATIVE BASELINE. The game has one continuing persistent world/history
rather than alternate gameplay save branches. The protagonist's higher-order progression is therefore
evaluated against what he has actually become in that single world.

AT.1 Transcendence qualification
   Each attainable protagonist Transcendence grade has a universal minimum personal-power /
    Existence foundation.
   Meeting the raw-power floor is necessary but not sufficient. Qualification also requires bespoke
    personal proofs generated from the protagonist's actual history, build and achievements.
   Valid proofs may involve Rank breakthroughs, Factors, Classes, skills, Authority, reality
    manipulation, survival, combat feats, knowledge, major world interactions, unique achievements
    and other genuinely causal development.
   The proof set is not a universal fixed quest checklist shared by every possible protagonist build.
   This prevents a weak protagonist from reaching Absolute through an obscure checkbox while also
    preventing one generic progression route from defining every playthrough.

AT.2 One persistent world and Absolute reachability
   There are no alternate ordinary save branches. The game is an ongoing persistent gacha
    world/history.
   Absolute remains theoretically reachable through sufficiently great long-term development in that
    continuing world.
   Earlier choices may make the route harder, longer or radically different, but the game must not
    silently and arbitrarily brick ultimate potential because the player chose a nonpreferred quest/build
    hundreds of hours earlier.
   Only a genuinely irreversible causal act may permanently foreclose a particular route to Absolute.
    Such an act must be meaningful in-world rather than a hidden game-design trap.
   The protagonist still Transcends only once; delaying the breakthrough preserves the possibility of
    qualifying for a higher final grade.

AT.3 Personal World Manifestation - no templates
   The protagonist has no predetermined canonical personal World Manifestation and does not select
    one from authored thematic templates.
   Its identity emerges from the protagonist that actually exists: Factors, developed Classes, learned
    skills, Existence Rank, repeated choices, formative experiences, worldview, powers, victories/losses,
    metaphysical contacts and other persistent causal state.
   Implementation may use reusable low-level rule, effect and visual primitives for feasibility, but those
    primitives may not reduce the outcome to a disguised preset inner-world template.
   Two sufficiently different long-running protagonist histories can therefore produce radically
    different personal World Manifestations.

AT.4 Post-unlock evolution
   Personal World Manifestation is persistent but not frozen when first obtained.

    efficiency, stability, counters and possible expressions.
   Evolution should preserve historical continuity: it develops from what the Manifestation already

AT.5 Combined Domain / personal Manifestation mastery
   A protagonist who possesses both personal World Manifestation and a Domain may develop

AV. Crown and Grand Class Architecture
STATUS: CURRENT AUTHORITATIVE BASELINE. This checkpoint refines the earlier Normal/Grand
model. Grand is not merely a slightly higher mastery tier; it is the unique metaphysical epitome/office of
a Class.

AV.1 Class recognition remains causal
   A Class remains an automatically recognized coherent mastery state produced by actual skills,
    training and demonstrated capability.
   Classes remain non-restrictive: they describe what the being has mastered rather than forbidding
    unrelated actions.
   Class recognition itself is useful. It can improve technique integration/efficiency, teaching/learning
    pathways, institutional/social recognition, specialized interactions and breakthrough access where
    those consequences follow from the mastery.

AV.2 Emergent and composite Classes
   If a being's capabilities no longer fit an existing Class, the system may recognize a genuinely new
    emergent Class with a stable identity rather than forcing the being into the nearest known category.
   Several parent Classes/skills/Factors may also form a specific composite Class when they become one
    coherent discipline.
   Emergent/composite recognition preserves parent mastery/history instead of erasing it.

AV.3 Crown Class - semi-Grand tier
   Working name: Crown Class.
   Crown is the non-unique apex of ordinary attainable mastery below Grand appointment.
   Several beings may simultaneously hold Crown recognition in the same underlying Class.
   A Crown bearer has reached the practical epitome of the discipline through personal mastery but
    does not yet possess the unique dimensional Grand mandate.
   Crown is the normal pool from which a Grand candidate can arise, although the dimensional
    defensive mechanism may consider compatibility, deeds, cosmological relevance and current
    existential needs in addition to mastery.

AV.4 Grand Class - one active bearer per Class
   For each canonical Class there is at most one active Grand bearer across the connected dimensional
    defensive order at a time.
   Grand is an appointment/recognition by the semi-conscious metaphysical defensive mechanism of
    the Dimensions, analogous to selecting a pillar/epitome capable of acting against final existential
    threats.
   The appointment cannot manufacture mastery from nothing. A Grand bearer must already embody
    extraordinary mastery of the underlying Class.
   Grand status is both recognition and real function: it may grant Class-appropriate dimensional
    Authority, defensive privileges, threat-specific capabilities, knowledge/access or other powers
    connected to that metaphysical office.
   Grand does not grant unrelated generic abilities merely because the title is prestigious.

AV.5 Vacancy, succession and loss
   Grand status can become vacant through death, resignation, incompatibility, dimensional
    revocation, successful succession or another explicit causal mechanism.
   A former Grand retains all independently learned skills, actual mastery, history and ordinary
    powers.
   Only powers/Authority belonging specifically to the Grand mandate disappear when the seat is lost
    unless an explicit mechanic made some portion intrinsic.
   A former Grand normally remains at least Crown-level in the underlying discipline unless their
    actual mastery/existence has been damaged or regressed.
   Multiple Grand candidates may therefore exist, but never multiple simultaneously appointed Grand
    bearers for the same Class.

AV.6 Emergent Classes and Grand eligibility
   A genuinely new Class is not automatically granted a Grand seat simply because it exists.
   If an emergent/composite Class becomes sufficiently coherent, developed and relevant to
    dimensional defense, the metaphysical system may eventually recognize a corresponding Grand
    seat.
   This keeps the class system effectively unbounded without flooding reality with meaningless Grand
    appointments.

AV.7 Attained mastery vs current expression
   Attained Class mastery/history and current practical expression are separate.
   If a swordswoman loses both arms, she does not forget or lose the attained sword Class merely
    because she cannot currently execute its normal body-dependent techniques.
   She may restore the original expression by regaining arms, use another mechanism such as
    prosthetic/energy limbs or telekinetic control, or evolve a derived version of the Class adapted to her
    new existence.
   A Class is truly lost only if the actual knowledge/mastery/existence supporting it is destroyed, erased
    or fundamentally transformed by a real mechanic.

AV.8 Knowledge and UI
   Exact Classes remain knowledge-dependent. Owned characters and the protagonist expose known
    state accurately; strangers may reveal only inferred disciplines until observation, appraisal, records,
    reputation or direct demonstration provides evidence.
   Concealment or misinformation can hide ordinary Class information where mechanically justified.
   A Grand appointment is much harder to conceal because it has dimensional significance, but
    sufficiently high-order concealment may still mask it if a real mechanic supports that result.
   The UI displays relevant Classes, Crown state and any Grand office without imposing equipped-class
    slots or a giant flat list.

AW. Ruler and Overlord Sovereignty
STATUS: CURRENT AUTHORITATIVE BASELINE. This checkpoint finalizes the universal Ruler-title
architecture. Ruler progression remains organic rather than a fixed numbered ladder.

AW.1 Universal title backbone
   There are only two universal sovereignty titles: Ruler and Overlord.
   Overlord is the highest universal sovereignty title. The game does not continue into an automatic
    Emperor / God-King / higher universal title ladder.
   Culture-, faction-, religion-, military-, story- and history-specific titles may exist in unlimited variety
    without becoming universal Ruler progression tiers.

AW.2 Ruler
   Ruler is the foundational sovereignty state.
   Ruler status requires real territorial sovereignty and enough personal power, Authority or
    equivalent capability to establish and maintain that control.
   Subordinates are not required. A solitary being can become a Ruler by holding Territory through
    personal strength or another valid mechanism.

AW.3 Overlord requires both personal capability and sovereignty
   Overlord cannot be obtained through sovereignty scale alone or through personal power alone.
   The personal side requires sufficiently high personal/existential power and effective Authority to
    support and impose high-order sovereignty.
   The sovereignty side requires control whose actual scale and metaphysical/strategic weight is
    Overlord-level.
   A personally weak figurehead with enormous nominal holdings does not qualify merely through
    subordinates. An absurdly strong lone fighter with only ordinary Territory also does not qualify
    merely through combat power.

AW.4 Overlord scale is structural, not a Domain count
   Several independent Domains can satisfy the sovereignty side, directly or through subordinate
    Rulers.
   A hierarchy combining direct and delegated Domains can satisfy it.
   A World-spanning sovereignty can satisfy it.
   One single contiguous Territory may satisfy it if the Territory genuinely spans most/all of a World or
    an entire Dimension and the Ruler actually controls it at that scale.
   Multi-world and cross-dimensional sovereignty can also qualify.
   Therefore losing all but one Domain does not automatically remove Overlord if the remaining
    sovereign structure is still Overlord-scale.

AW.5 No Overlord sub-rank ladder, but enormous internal variation
   Overlord is not divided into universal numbered ranks or mandatory higher titles.

   Different Overlords can nevertheless differ by orders of magnitude in personal Existence Rank,

   The UI/AI may summarize this as Overlord Standing, Scope or Threat Estimate, but this is descriptive

AW.7 Joint sovereignty

   Several beings may all be active Overlords over the same high-order structure if each personally
    meets the power/Authority requirement and each genuinely possesses co-sovereign control.
   A subordinate, regent, administrator or symbolic co-ruler does not gain Overlord merely by
    association.
   Joint Overlords may divide Domains, Authority channels, military command or metaphysical
    responsibilities according to the actual structure.

AW.8 Knowledge and presentation
   The Ruler interface presents concrete sovereignty facts, Authority, Territories/Domains, subordinate
    structure and relevant titles rather than a single Ruler-rank bar.
   Current title, historical title and estimated Overlord standing are knowledge-dependent for unknown
    foreign entities.
   Local political titles can be displayed alongside the universal Ruler/Overlord state without being
    mistaken for higher universal power tiers.

AX. Final Architecture Freeze
STATUS: FROZEN FOR TUNING AND CODE RECONCILIATION. This checkpoint records the Creative
Director's final broad architecture/production answers. Unspecified subordinate implementation/design
glue is delegated to the engineering/design assistant. Future changes are explicit revisions rather than
routine open-question discovery.

AX.1 Civilization advancement identity correction
   Civilization advancement does not mean convergence toward modern Earth cities, industrialism or
    contemporary urban design.
   Each civilization becomes more capable, grand, complex and mature along its own authored genre,
    metaphysics, technology, culture and visual language.
   A cultivation/wuxia civilization becomes a greater cultivation civilization; magical, draconic,
    eldritch, elven, undead, divine, cyber, post-apocalyptic and other civilizations deepen their own line
    rather than being normalized into one endpoint.
   Cross-cultural borrowing is causal and selective: a civilization may reject, reinterpret, ritualize,
    biologize, magicalize or hybridize imported knowledge without losing its identity.
   Only actual history, conquest, deliberate hybridization, cultural transformation or player/NPC
    choices redirect a civilization's development line.

AX.2 Ruler Mode information architecture
   Five primary portrait-mode destinations: Home, Characters, Gacha, Territory, Records.
   Territory is the action/sovereignty destination and internally separates Territory, Domain/Core,
    Dispatch, Projects, armies/war, logistics/resources, subordinate requests and strategic map controls.
   Records contains distinct internal tabs for Reports, Chronicle, Codex and Intelligence.
   Strategic map remembers the last view/overlay and exposes a one-tap return to Main Territory.
   Character pages group multiple Manifestations under one Character Identity and expose their
    divergent builds/routes instead of listing them as unrelated duplicates.
   Home remains deliberately character/lobby focused and low-information. World/Domain styling
    may subtly adapt while interaction grammar remains stable.
   Reports may generate real Android notifications. Known scheduled events can use local scheduling;
    unpredictable background events use best-effort Android background work under OS restrictions.
    Notification acknowledgement never changes world state.

AX.3 World Mode HUD, difficulty and controls
   Default navigation uses a small knowledge-limited minimap; full maps remain knowledge
    projections rather than omniscient completion screens.
   HUD fades outside relevance and expands for combat, low HP, hazards, lock-on, traversal and
    interaction.
   Damage numbers are visible by default and customizable.
   Enemy HP/state precision is knowledge-dependent; unknown beings may expose only approximate
    condition.
   Interaction prompts remain minimal/contextual rather than icon clutter.
   There is one canonical global difficulty. No Easy/Normal/Hard profile and no hidden player-level
    rubber-banding.
