### 7★ Systems decisions that must not disappear
- **Product:** Offline-first Android single-player Ruler/Overlord RPG simulating a living gacha world without a mandatory public backend. Same SQLite-centered authoritative history for portrait strategic **Ruler Mode** and landscape embodied **World Mode**; Unreal is the one game runtime. Protagonist starts essentially with nothing. World Actor presentation does not replace authoritative simulated entities.
- **Open World:** effectively extensible region graph, authentic first 100×100km accepted World anchor, navigation and physical causality; maps/knowledge must be earned, may be inaccurate, and change over time. Meaningful exploration, real structures/interiors, source-grounded equipment/loot, factions/territories and deterministic procedural generation with authored anchors. No arbitrary invisible wall when physics/capability permits traversal; no forced battle at first spawn. Local environment and active NPCs use appropriate high fidelity; distant populations/wars/ecology use lazy compressed event-driven simulation, not thousands of always-ticking AI actors.
- **Persistence:** no routine reset or season wipe; canonical event history and meaningful WorldState survive gameplay, death, ownership, Ruler changes, war, ecology, construction, equipment/wardrobe damage and offline absence. Real outcomes are not forgiven by rubber-band catch-up. Mobile game saves must be durable, transactional/migratable and local.
- **Ruler/Overlord (latest v0.36 AW):** only universal sovereignty titles Ruler and Overlord, no compulsory Emperor/God-King ladder or Overlord sub-ranks. Ruler requires actual territory and sufficient personal Authority/power. Overlord requires BOTH genuinely Overlord-scale sovereignty AND personal/existential Authority; one truly world/dimension-scale unified Domain can qualify, not an arbitrary minimum count. Joint real co-sovereignty is possible if all qualify; active title may end after genuine loss while peak historical status survives. Rank/Authority/Classes/Factors/Transcendence are distinct systems and cannot be collapsed into GP power labels.
- **Character ownership and evolution (v0.36 AP):** `Identity` groups authored Versions; multiple independently persistent copies are `Manifestations`, not extra Character identities. Copies develop different routes, skills, equipment and assignments. A true Grand Convergence explicitly fuses chosen Manifestations and preserves source histories; no automatic duplicate-counter consumption. Current Rarity promotion and route families are modular/extensible; no whole-character per-Version animation duplication. Grand Class appointment (v0.36 AV) is a distinct high-order concept: one active seat per canonical Class across the connected dimensional mechanism, Crown below; never conflate with the one character's **Grand Convergence** form.
- **Combat rules (v0.36 H/Q/AH and later approved Luminous v4):** original shared authoritative effect/stat/status/Authority core with **different execution/timing** for real-time World action, continuous action-value strategic turn, scalable abstract war, and boss-authorized hybrid phases. Ruler Mode strategic front six + reserve replacements, potentially 12–18 total; turn automation where applicable. World Mode real Ruler + up to two immediate companions and near-instant switching except authored commitments, bespoke entry/QTE overlap. Touch input has left movement joystick and right camera drag, permanent jump/dodge, character-specific normals/skills/parries/cancel windows and meters, direct tiered Ultimates and World Fantasm. Attack and skill availability must be real; consequences commit on authentic authored animation/physical contact, not fake HP or a timer. Physical location, free traversal, hostility, chase, terrain and damage persist during combat. No universal glowing parry indicator, no generic perfect-dodge reward, no global guard/hold-heavy, no universal forced dismount; no ordinary combat inventory consumables or Ruler Commands in World action mode. **Hybrid boss phase transitions are encounter-driven**, not a universal unrestricted player-controlled toggle. All AoE follows exact authored affected-target scope without an arbitrary six-target cap.
- **Mature content (v0.36 AA/AB/AE + later approved production visibility semantics):** first-class core scope includes canonically adult sexual interaction, nudity, violence, blood/gore, injury, breakable weapons/armor, garment tear/removal, equipment and body states; the presentation follows species/anatomy/physical state. Explicit sexual participation is between canonically adult, voluntarily participating characters; Ruler contract/Authority does not substitute for consent. Do not impose global affection scores, romantic quest/gift/story completion, arbitrary rarity/rank progression, content tags or imported animation QA legacy flags as new sexual-access gates. Romance and adult content are separate; one does not imply the other. There is no universal stat buff for sex; authored character-specific effects and persistent biography/history are possible. SFW/privacy presentation (where player-selected) affects the **rendered view only**; it must never secretly alter canonical ownership, clothing, injury, inventory, completed action or source animation authority. Reproduction is not a universal simulator. Authored scenes are modular and can be replayed in the gallery consistent with provenance and original availability rules. General mature-combat violence remains physically integrated; no cosmetic-only independent HP/durability ledger.
- **Procedural systems and computation (v0.36 AE):** execute expensive logic only when a meaningful event requires it. No always-on ordinary-food-web, continuous offscreen NPC daily scheduling, mount-breeding simulator, generic continuous biological aging, synthetic affection grind, universal infinite structure destruction or irrelevant micro-management. Delegate solved farming and legitimate repeatable jobs through actual characters/orders; no stamina gate or skip-ticket economy.
- **Gacha and World Director:** local real in-world contracts and distinct Manifestations, recorded pity/currency, no paid public service requirement, Source/banners/currency and content must obey canon. Autonomous Director may schedule approved sealed packages/surprise without manufacturing anti-player enemies/currency or rewriting legitimate history.
- **UI:** native premium Gate12 Ruler interface must be a whole portrait strategy RPG (not a World HUD rotated), with roster, summon, inventory, territory, diplomacy, codex, quests, settings, developer reporting and other approved sub-systems. Landscape World HUD must support actual touch control, combat, movement modes and interactable embodied actors. Input/UX never creates a parallel game-state authority. The current older Gate10 UI was dismissed; reuse accepted nine visual reference scopes and later approvals where available.
- **Animation/media production:** original source 3D/2D/animations, character rigs and source art remain immutable approval baselines. Rig-family/character-independent **shared motion catalog**; semantic actions, physical contact frames, morphs/face, skeletal derivative compatibility, multi-actor role tracks, cancellation and state recovery are data-driven. Importer/retargeting may generate genuine technical derivatives, but never silently approve artistry or fabricate source clips.
