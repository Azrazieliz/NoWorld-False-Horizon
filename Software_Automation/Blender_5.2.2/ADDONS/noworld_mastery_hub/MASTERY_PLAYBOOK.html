Designed for 8 GB RAM / i3-1115G4 with Blender 5.2.2, Unreal 5.8.3, Gaea 2.3.1, and Koikatsu input. All production asset generation requires a distinct commission.

Fastest reliable automation principles

1. Work from high-quality sources

Use licensed mocap, scanned PBR, curated shapes, dedicated add-ons and asset libraries; automate import and adaptation rather than drawing every key or rebuilding each character.

2. Maintain source parameter systems

Use modifier stack, Geometry Nodes group interfaces, material mask node groups, reusable corrective shape keys and Asset Browser catalogs. No destructive edits to canonical source.

3. Let the right engine do the job

Blender authors and bakes; Unreal does Control Rig, retarget, gameplay notifies, Niagara and Android quality. Gaea provides eroded terrain; Koikatsu preserves identity and original face assets.

4. Remove meaningless manual clicking

Native bpy/RNA, background batch, reusable CLI manifests, named controls and validated add-ons take precedence over fragile pixel-coordinate clicks.

5. Check final fidelity

Automated topology/action checks are necessary but cannot evaluate expressive performance, refined design, toon shaders or native game playback. Artistic comparison remains a separate gate.

6. Safe by default

The installed hub is read-only. It doesn't create meshes, play animations into canonical files, retarget into Gate 12 or overwrite frozen project assets.

Known limitations

Installed native Blender, CloudRig and Retarget extensions do not automatically guarantee high-end rigging. Experimental cloth/hair physics are not equivalent to Unreal or KK runtime. CMU mocap retarget to the Koikatsu sample failed visual pose inspection even though native UE assets were generated. Gaea 2.3.1 Swarm failed in the remote process console. Mastery is not certified until those workflows become reproducible, context-correct and artistically accepted.
