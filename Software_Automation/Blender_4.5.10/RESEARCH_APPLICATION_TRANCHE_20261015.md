# Research-to-production adoption tranche — professional standards, 2026-10-15

Status: independently checked source claims and tested selected capabilities in native Blender 4.5.10. **Not exhaustive study** of full books or paid courses. Never report a paywalled video as watched.

## Reliable external knowledge and what it changes

1. **Blender Studio Toon Character Workflow** — https://studio.blender.org/training/toon-character-workflow/ (28 videos, 3h41; chapters concept art → modeling → shape keys → rigging/weight painting → toon shading/outline → animation). Course index examined; paid videos not accessed. **Application:** character close-up must demonstrate face deformation and line-art shading, not simply import static model.
2. **Blender Studio Character Animation course and free Graph Editor sections** — https://studio.blender.org/training/character-animation/ . Individual free videos cover timing curves, selective controllers, keyframes, NLA/pose workflows; course structure reviewed, no false watch claims. **Application:** genuine 72-frame editable pose and facial acting timeline rather than sequential still images, curve-interpolated camera motion.
3. **Blender Studio Stylized Character Workflow pose polishing** — https://studio.blender.org/training/stylized-character-workflow/5d66533123bda402f76bd3f7/ . Artists bake rig deformations into shape keys and sculpt manual fixes; automatic weights alone are insufficient. **Application:** acceptance must require posed deformation and close-up correction passes for character/creature.
4. **Blender Studio Sprite Fright style philosophy** — https://studio.blender.org/blog/sprite-fright-the-illusion-of-simplicity/ . Shape appeal and selective meaningful detail are more important than indiscriminately noisy geometry. **Application:** architectural visual hierarchy: strong facade outlines and arch/door rhythm with localized glints, ivy, signs, fine material texture. **Still:** current sanctuary is stylized diorama, not film-quality realistic archviz.
5. **Blender Studio Sprite Fright animation style guide summary** — https://studio.blender.org/blog/sprite-fright-style-guide/ . Mainly on twos, 1s for quick movement, smears and strong posing, facial expression choices. **Application planned:** true hybrid handdrawn style test must not be reduced to a moving 3D camera.
6. **Blender Studio procedural material customization** — https://studio.blender.org/blog/new-training-customizing-material-assets/ . Reusable shader node groups with artist-facing controls. **Application tested:** editable two-tone asymmetric same-mesh material group on KK-derived hair/skirt; preserving textures and local/world blend.
7. **Blender Studio 4.5 Fundamentals course** — https://studio.blender.org/training/blender-fundamentals-45-lts/ . Covers modeling, sculpting, modifiers, UVs, Grease Pencil, compositor, video editing, rigging, textures, Eevee/Cycles and color management. **Application:** version-matched source categorization and real Cycles 4.5 light/material tests.
8. **Blender Studio 4.5 Shading Theory lesson** — https://studio.blender.org/training/blender-fundamentals-45-lts/blender_4-5_lts_shading-theory/ . Principled physically-based roughness, material preview vs scene world light. **Application:** architecture layered noise/textures/bump, three-point lighting and real Cycles render rather than Workbench-only proof.
9. **PBRT v4 book (Pharr/Jakob/Humphreys; freely readable online)** — https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Textures_and_Materials ; https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Textures_and_Materials/Material_Interface_and_Implementations . Texture functions separate spatial color/roughness variation from BSDF; bump mapping gives apparent detail without adding geometric triangles. **Application:** stone, slate, foliage shader uses geometry-free noise color variation and bump; material looks must still be inspected closely. Book read selected chapter sections, **not entire book**.
10. **NVIDIA GPU Gems 3 chapter 6 wind trees** — https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-i-geometry/chapter-6-gpu-generated-procedural-wind-animations-trees . Wind motion with trunk/branch stiffness and phase stochasticity on GPU instead of bone-per-leaf. **Application planned:** artist-authored tree trunk/branch hierarchy and UE-compatible GPU wind, still unimplemented; current trees have simplistic cone foliage.
11. **NVIDIA GPU Gems 3 chapter 16 Crysis vegetation** — https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-iii-rendering/chapter-16-vegetation-procedural-animation-and-shading-crysis . Wind fields and vegetation-specific shading and local interactions. **Application planned:** hierarchical deformation and lower-overhead LOD, not yet executed.
12. **Pixar in a Box / Khan Academy** — https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/art-of-lighting and https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/pixar-rigging . Free curricula on lighting roles, color scripts, rig deformation and acting. **Application:** dedicated material/lighting QA vs mere skeleton or material import.
13. **KK Blender Porter Pack shader breakdown** — https://kkbpwiki.github.io/material ; https://kkbpwiki.github.io/material_breakdown_advanced . Saturation functions and separate light/dark color, alphas for hair/eyelines/body, different eye and face shading. **Application:** discovered alpha-linked eyeline textures in current assembled GLB, but 4.5 Cycles render has black eyelash/eyes defects. Full in-game likeness not accepted.
14. **Open Blender demo files** — https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/ . CC0 human base meshes 49 MB, CC-BY character poses and open movie demos; feasible supervised dissection source, respect license, architecture and hardware. **Not downloaded or integrated yet**.
15. **Wikibooks Blender 3D: Noob to Pro** — https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Blender_3D%3A_Noob_to_Pro . Historical open book; current printable edition specifically warns it is not a reliable general introduction to modern Blender. **Use only background concepts, never authoritative 4.5 API/control instructions**.
16. **Grease Pencil 4.5 native animation** — https://docs.blender.org/UATEST/manual/en/4.5/grease_pencil/animation/introduction.html . Key drawings, onion skin, transform animation and cutout-style inherited animation. **Application executed earlier:** 48 frames, 14 drawn frames, final MP4; next goal refined handdrawn character continuity/clean linework.
17. **Studio animation style constraint:** https://studio.blender.org/blog/sprite-fright-sculpting-advice-for-production/ . Designs require shot-specific expression/angle tests and limitations passed to later departments. **Application:** future character review must include shot-specific face, eye material and motion artifact tests before professional acceptance.

## Executed visual and movement change

Procedural Cycles stone/roof/foliage textures and color/roughness variation tested. A 72-frame native camera animation is available inside editable .blend, renderable to MP4. Base sandbox remains untouched. This is a **substantially more detailed artistic study**, not a realistic high-budget building.

Private KK-derived assembly has 121 bones, 6 mesh parts and 1,797 part-wise morph channels. Animation test adds three exclusive facial channels, five skeleton controls, keyframed camera and secondary ornamental motes across 72 frames. Original source immutable, no authorized repo content. Close-up exposes eye/eyelash alpha or shader artifacts; hold artistic approval.
