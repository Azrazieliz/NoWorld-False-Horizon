# Mandatory refined-art production acceptance standard — NoWorld: False Horizon

**Authority:** User's 2026-10-15 direction. This applies to every Blender mastery workflow, not just present prototypes. **Never equate software execution or functioning exporters with artistic mastery.** The objective is a repeatable system for extremely refined, individually authored, stylistically coherent, source-editable and performant builds, produced autonomously with minimal human technical operation.

## Required independent gates

1. **Technical implementation:** Native versioned Blender execution, controlled parameters, recoverable and editable sources, deterministic reconstruction, UV and topology checks, constraints and export behavior.
2. **Visual/artistic excellence:** Evaluated at close-up and full-body/mid/far range against approved artistic references, coherent silhouette and composition, high-quality shapes, refined linework/anatomy/design, intentional material detail and lighting. Technical execution cannot pass this gate.
3. **Adaptability and reusability:** Meaningful user-level controls, including asymmetry, within-object and across-object gradients, material layering and region independence, geometry variations, progressive damage, mesh fit/collision, optional components, material texture revisions and non-destructive regeneration. Check at least three visually distinct parameter variants with no accidental loss of identity.
4. **Performance and downstream parity:** Native Unreal export/import and actual evaluated assets, correct normals/UVs/morphs/rig animation/baked textures/LOD/collision, transparent known parity losses, and appropriate Android validation when relevant. A .fbx path alone does not pass.
5. **Professional production usability:** Source organization, semantically named bones and materials, predictable parameter effects, reversible edits, fast previews vs high-quality finals, reproducible generation, an annotated creative control guide and rights/provenance.
6. **Human visual signoff / Gate authority:** For game-canonical designs, creative direction requires Gate 00 or the responsible production Gate's approval. Technical mastery experiments may validate engineering independently but do not approve canonical art.

## Domain-specific refinement

**Anime character and creature:** Non-template face structure, intentional eye/eyelid/nose/mouth geometry, expressive asymmetry, silhouette and hair flow fidelity, cloth fold logic, anatomically coherent multi-angle sculpt/topology, smooth skin weighting with pose-dependent correctives, purposeful accents, animation-ready clothing and accessories. For nonhumans, species-specific skeleton proportions, articulation, behavior, gait, tail/wing membranes and ground interaction. Existing primitive creature is not accepted.

**Architectural assets and worlds:** Believable real construction and structural language, modular variation with consistent spatial scale, facade depth/detail, stone/wood/fabric/glass response, calibrated wear and roughness, genuinely navigable interiors, believable materials, overdraw/draw-call and LOD planning. A cube-and-arch assembly is only a foundation.

**Animations/cinematics:** Foot contact and joints are validated technically AND timing, anticipation, inertia, secondary animation, balance, facial acting, camera staging, expressive beats, multi-character interaction, shot and narrative continuity meet animator quality review. Smooth curves or contact lock alone are insufficient.

**2D anime and hybrid work:** Premium hand-drawn 2D anime/manga finish; clean, deliberate line quality, consistent character drawing on changing angles, impact posing and selected hand-authored corrections, crisp cel shading with occasional painterly support, cinematic composition and believable moving backgrounds. No CGI-like/3D finish to replace requested pure 2D. Automated interpolation is a tool, not a visual-quality guarantee.

**Materials/textures:** Real editable gradients inside individual regions and across objects, asymmetric multi-axis distributions and independent masks, damaged/torn clothing controls, physically coherent fabric/stone/skin/hair response as required, automatic bake/parity checks in Unreal; do not mistake two flat colored objects for a gradient.

**Koikatsu reconstruction:** Retain identity, high-quality faces, textures, hair cards, independent materials/eyes/eyelashes and accessories, rig/expressions/clothing state, mod-dependent behavior, provenance; compare screenshots side-by-side in Koikatsu, Blender, Unreal, including animated poses. Successful GLB load alone is not reconstruction.

## Acceptance evidence per demonstration
Every family of the mandated 25 demos must include a meaningful refined asset, editable .blend and generator/settings, close/mid/full visual records, technical QA (where appropriate), comparisons against reference or source, resolution of known failure reports, Unreal/Android records when the requirement calls for them, recorded performance and explicit artistic acceptance. If any is absent, label the demonstration **technical prototype**, **partial** or **failed** — never completed or production-ready.

## Current truth
As of 2026-10-15, 0/25 professional artistic demo targets have been independently accepted. Native Blender and Unreal assets, readable Koikatsu source shards and numerically constrained IK gait are genuine technical milestones only. None authorizes an unqualified 'mastery complete' declaration.

## Automation directive
The user supplies designs and approvals, not routine Blender operations. The agent must operate Blender and maintain the underlying scripts and references, prioritize native API/headless batch execution, and avoid risking another production Gate or live Unreal project.
